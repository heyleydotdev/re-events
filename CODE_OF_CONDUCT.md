# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in ReEvents to be respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code and kind to people.
- Assume good intent, ask questions, and explain reasoning.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and any project space.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately through the repository's security advisory channel and mark the report for the maintainers. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and take proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Consequences may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces.
